{% comment %} ========================================================================== The site footer. One include, so every page that has a footer has this one. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- It is built for Django templates and plain CSS: the React/GSAP footer it is modelled on has no equivalent here, so what was ported is the design — the ambient glow, the masked grid, the giant brand text, the tilted marquee, the glass pills and the curtain reveal — and the behaviour was written vanilla in js/components.js (scroll reveal, magnetic pills on a fine pointer, the curtain's scroll progress). The curtain: .site-footer__stage below is a 100dvh box in normal flow that clips what it holds, and .site-footer inside it is fixed to the bottom of the viewport. The page scrolls the box up and the footer is uncovered — never a moment before. It needs two things from the page, both declared in css/components.css and both checked by tools/pdp_probe.py:
has to be opaque and stacked above the footer, and no sheet may force it transparent. Below 660px of viewport height the curtain is dropped and the footer returns to normal flow, because a fixed 100dvh footer would clip its own content out of reach there. Two deliberate omissions: * No newsletter form. The old one posted to the current URL and nothing in the project ever handled it. * No link columns and no duplicate pills. The store, the account links and favourites were a second copy of navigation the header already carries; the chips below are the actions that are not reachable anywhere else. Links are real routes.
