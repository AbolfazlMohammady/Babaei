{% comment %} ========================================================================== The site footer. One include, so every page that has a footer has this one. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- It is built for Django templates and plain CSS: the React/GSAP footer it is modelled on has no equivalent here, so what was ported is the design — the ambient glow, the masked grid, the giant brand text, the tilted marquee, the glass pills and the credits bar — and the behaviour was written vanilla in js/components.js (scroll reveal, magnetic pills on a fine pointer, back to top). Two deliberate differences from the original: * No "curtain" reveal. The original pins the footer to the viewport behind a clipped wrapper; that needs every page's `main` to be opaque, and on a short viewport it clips the footer's own content out of reach. The footer sits in normal flow instead, so it works at any height on every page. * No newsletter form. The old one posted to the current URL and nothing in the project ever handled it. Links are real routes. Instagram, Telegram and YouTube have no URL anywhere in this project — they were `#` in the old footer too — so they stay markers until the store's handles are known. Nothing here invents an address or a handle. ========================================================================== {% endcomment %}
